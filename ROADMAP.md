# Roadmap

Russian version: [ROADMAP.ru.md](ROADMAP.ru.md)

## What v1.0 means

**v1.0 = two to four players can play the whole Far Cry campaign, Training to Volcano, without the console and without the developer nearby.** Milestone: [v1.0](https://github.com/Bantila/FarCryCoop/milestone/1).

Versions on the way:

| Version | Condition |
|---|---|
| `v0.3-beta` | Current build, if the level sweep passes on at least 90% of the 20 levels |
| `v0.x-beta` | Items 1 to 9 below are closed one by one |
| `v1.0` | The two-PC test and the full playthrough pass, and no crash is open |

## After 1.0

Milestone: [After 1.0](https://github.com/Bantila/FarCryCoop/milestone/2). None of these blocks 1.0.

- Client animation for AI jumps and other animation-driven AI actions: [#16](https://github.com/Bantila/FarCryCoop/issues/16)
- Joining without Steam or a VPN (join code, UDP hole punching, relay): [#17](https://github.com/Bantila/FarCryCoop/issues/17)
- Host admin panel in game, mission select by any player, friendly-fire switch: [#18](https://github.com/Bantila/FarCryCoop/issues/18), plan in [games/far-cry/PLAN-fun-mode.md](games/far-cry/PLAN-fun-mode.md)
- Player list during the game in the launcher.

## Later: other games of the series

The owner's plan is to finish Far Cry 1 first, then pick Far Cry 3, 5 or 6. Each game has its own repository.
